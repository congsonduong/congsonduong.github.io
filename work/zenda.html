Listings that get found.

Keyword research, trend spotting and product design for a fan-driven, design-led print-on-demand catalogue on Amazon.

01The challenge

On Amazon, a product nobody can find doesn't exist. Search is the shop window.

Print-on-demand categories are crowded with near-identical designs. To win, a product needs demand that's already there, a design that fills a gap, and a listing written in the words shoppers actually type.

02What I did

01

Keyword research

Used keyword research tools to find search terms with real demand, then built them into titles, bullet points, descriptions and backend search terms across the catalogue.

02

Trend and gap research

Ran tool-led research into design trends and category gaps, and turned them into new product ideas for a fan-driven catalogue.

03

Mockups

Produced product mockups in Photoshop and Illustrator so every listing looked ready to buy.

04

Listing operations

Listed and maintained products in Amazon Seller Central.

03Creative

One design system applied across products, plus the listing image set shoppers see first.

04What it taught me

Fans search for the reference. They type the character, the quote or the in-joke, then "shirt". So I wrote the listings in their words.

The image does half the job. The keywords get a product seen. The mockup decides whether anyone clicks.
