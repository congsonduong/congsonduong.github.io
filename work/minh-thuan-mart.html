All work Retail growth case study

Turning one-off shoppers into regulars.

A content calendar, small paid campaigns and personal follow-up that grew a local store's audience, repeat customers and daily sales.

CompanyMinh Thuan Mart
RoleSales & Marketing Assistant
PeriodMay 2021 – Mar 2023
ChannelsFacebook, email, in-store
Avg daily sales+12%

01Context

A neighbourhood store in Haiphong, Vietnam, competing with bigger chains for the same shoppers.

The store had loyal regulars but no planned marketing. Social posts went out irregularly, promotions weren't announced ahead of time, and there was no follow-up with customers after they bought.

02The challenge

Grow the audience, bring customers back more often, and lift daily sales on a small-business budget.

There was no room for big media spend. Every result had to come from consistency, relevance and the personal touch a small store can offer and a chain can't.

03What I did

01

A planned content calendar

Regular Facebook posts built around seasonal moments and product categories, so the page had a rhythm people could follow.

02

Small, focused paid campaigns

Modest paid boosts behind the posts and promotions most likely to bring people into the store.

03

Personalised recommendations

Product suggestions based on what each customer actually bought, in store and in follow-up messages.

04

Email and direct follow-up

Follow-up timed alongside the content calendar for seasonal and category promotions.

04Creative

Posts from the content calendar: seasonal promotions, product spotlights and the page's cover banner. I designed all of them.

05Outcome

+1,000Facebook followers in six months
+15%repeat customers
+12%average daily sales
The regulars didn't need an ad. They needed someone to remember what they bought last time.

06What I learned

Posting on a plan worked better than boosting at random. The follower growth came from showing up regularly, with small paid pushes behind posts that were already doing well.

Remembering people sells. Recommendations based on what someone bought last time were what brought them back. It is audience segmentation, done by hand at the till.
