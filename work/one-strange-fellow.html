A gaming YouTube channel built from scratch, where every thumbnail, title and opening line was treated like ad creative.

01The challenge

No subscribers, no budget and no algorithm history. Just a thumbnail and a title competing in a crowded category.

On YouTube, the thumbnail and title do the same job as a paid ad's first frame. If they don't earn the click, nothing else matters. A new channel has no audience to fall back on.

02What I did

01

Research what already works

Studied the videos that were performing in the gaming category and built concepts from proven angles instead of guesses.

02

Scripts that hold attention

Wrote scripts designed to keep viewers past the opening seconds, the same skill as a paid video's first three seconds.

03

Thumbnails and titles as a test

Designed every thumbnail in Photoshop and wrote every title, testing hooks and angles against the category winners.

04

One repeatable workflow

Treated idea → script → edit → thumbnail → title as one audience journey, so each new video was quicker to make and better than the last.

03Creative

Thumbnails designed for the channel. Each one is built to be read in under a second at phone size.
